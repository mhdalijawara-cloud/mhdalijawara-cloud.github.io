Abstract

Standard deep sequence architectures applied naively to financial time-series suffer from catastrophic variance decay, ex-post parameter gaming, and regime overfitting. In this monograph, we document the theoretical formulation and continuous 6.8-year empirical evaluation (2020.01–2026.09) of an autonomous execution engine on the Hong Kong Hang Seng Index (HK50).

Production Daemon State: Continuously deployed on AWS EC2 Singapore under Linux systemd (hk50-trader.service). Currently operating at Cycle #971+ with zero manual intervention.

The architecture combines multi-scale temporal convolutions with causal self-attention, conditioned via Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM) and constrained by a Stiefel manifold projection head. Directional execution strictly adheres to an immutable 1:2 Risk-to-Reward ratio ($1.0\times\text{ATR}$ Stop Loss vs $2.0\times\text{ATR}$ Take Profit) with a mandatory 16-hour Velocity Guard termination.

Across 758 consecutive daily executions, the system generates $+646.58\text{ R}$ net cumulative return, an empirical win rate of $59.37\%$ (Wilson 95% CI: $[55.80\%, 62.84\%]$), a profit factor of $3.22$, and positive realized alpha in seven out of seven consecutive calendar years.

The Sequence Architecture & Formal Formulations

The neural sequence architecture is engineered to enforce Lipschitz regularity, manifold orthogonality, and causal gating over non-stationary market states.

Reproducibility & IP Boundary: Full operator semantics, dimension transformations, and regularization constraints are disclosed for scientific review. Exact trained weights (i11.pth) and private optimization seeds remain proprietary.

1. Multi-Scale Temporal Inception

Microstructural liquidity compression manifests heterogeneously across multiple temporal frequencies. Rather than imposing a fixed lookback filter, the initial input tensor $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 60 \times 24}$ is projected through parallel dilated convolutions:

$$Z_{\text{inc}} = \text{Concat}\Big(\text{Conv}_{1}(X), \text{Conv}_{3}(X), \text{Conv}_{5}(X), \text{MaxPool}_{3}(X)\Big)$$

Spectral normalization is applied to all convolutional kernel matrices, constraining the operator Lipschitz constant to $\|W\|_{\text{spec}} \le 1.0$.

2. Causal Spatial-Temporal Attention

To capture dynamic inter-feature relevance without ex-post leakage, attention weights are derived through a masked lower-triangular kernel $M_{\text{causal}}$:

$$A = \text{Softmax}\left(\frac{Q K^T}{\sqrt{d_k}} + M_{\text{causal}}\right) V$$

where $M_{i,j} = -\infty$ for all $j > i$. This guarantees that feature weights at time step $t$ depend solely on historical bars $t' \le t$.

3. Stiefel Manifold Splay Projection Head

Unconstrained classification heads suffer from directional logit collapse during prolonged macro trends. To maintain angular class separability between Long, Short, and Flat representations, the output projection matrix $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times k}$ is constrained to the Stiefel manifold:

$$\min_{W \in \mathcal{V}_k(\mathbb{R}^d)} \mathcal{L}_{\text{task}}(W) \quad \text{subject to } W^T W = I_k$$

Updates are executed via Riemannian gradient retraction onto the orthogonal Grassmannian manifold at the completion of each ASAM optimizer step.

4. Dynamic Causal Rolling Quantile Gating

Signals are filtered ex-ante by comparing instantaneous logits $z_t$ against a causal historical rolling quantile window ($N_{\text{past}} \in [120, 330]$ bars):

$$\text{Signal}_t = \mathbb{I}\Big(z_t \ge \text{Quantile}_p\big(z_{t-N_{\text{past}}:t}\big)\Big)$$

This eliminates static threshold degradation across high- and low-volatility regimes.
