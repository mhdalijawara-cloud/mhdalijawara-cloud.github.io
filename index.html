Quantitative Philosophy

Causal Invariance & Anti-Gaming Principles

Standard deep learning architectures applied naively to financial time series suffer from high-frequency memorization and objective gaming. Our models enforce four non-negotiable structural constraints:

  • Zero Look-Ahead Conditioning: Feature transformations rely strictly on causal historical rolling quantiles ($N_{\text{past}} \in [120, 330]$ bars).
  • Immutable 1:2 R:R Payoff: Eliminating arbitrary profit-taking; trades strictly terminate at $1.0\times\text{ATR}$ stop or $2.0\times\text{ATR}$ target.
  • Hard Velocity Guard: Floating positions terminate mark-to-market at hour 16, eliminating multi-day overnight decay.
  • Stiefel Splay Manifold: Orthogonal feature projections preventing directional collapse during market regime transitions.
