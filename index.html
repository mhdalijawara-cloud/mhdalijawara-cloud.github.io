The Sequence Architecture & Formal Formulations

The sequence modeling pipeline transforms raw limit order flow features $X \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 60 \times 24}$ into discrete directional decisions through four regularized stages.Scientific Disclosure Note: Network topologies, mathematical loss bounds, and empirical ledger entries are published for peer replication. Proprietary neural checkpoint weights and private optimization seeds remain confidential.

1. Multi-Scale Temporal Inception

Microstructural volatility compression manifests across disparate frequency bands. To extract invariant representations without manual timeframe selection, parallel 1D dilated convolutions process the input simultaneously:

$$Z_{\text{inc}} = \text{Concat}\Big(\text{Conv}_{1}(X), \text{Conv}_{3}(X), \text{Conv}_{5}(X), \text{MaxPool}_{3}(X)\Big)$$

Spectral normalization is applied to all convolutional kernel matrices, constraining the operator Lipschitz constant to $\|W\|_{\text{spec}} \le 1.0$.

2. Causal Spatial-Temporal Attention

Feature relevance is non-stationary. To identify leading indicators during pre-market auction transitions without look-ahead bias, causal lower-triangular masking is strictly enforced:

$$A = \text{Softmax}\left(\frac{Q K^T}{\sqrt{d_k}} + M_{\text{causal}}\right) V$$

where $M_{i,j} = -\infty$ for all $j > i$. Attention weights at step $t$ depend solely on past observations $t' \le t$.

3. Stiefel Manifold Splay Projection Head

Unconstrained linear projection heads suffer from directional logit collapse during sustained macro trends. To enforce geometric angular separation between Long, Short, and Flat signal spaces, projection matrix $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times k}$ is constrained to the Stiefel manifold:Orthogonal retraction on $\mathcal{V}_k(\mathbb{R}^d)$ prevents directional collapse and maintains maximal angular distance $\theta_{ij} \approx \frac{2\pi}{3}$ between competing action classes.

$$\min_{W \in \mathcal{V}_k(\mathbb{R}^d)} \mathcal{L}_{\text{task}}(W) \quad \text{subject to } W^T W = I_k$$

4. Causal Rolling Quantile Gating

To eliminate ex-post selection bias, execution gating thresholds $q_t$ are evaluated strictly over a causal historical lookback window of $N_{\text{past}} = 300$ trading bars:

$$q_t = \text{Quantile}\Big(\big\{p_{t-k}\big\}_{k=1}^{N_{\text{past}}}, \tau = 0.90\Big)$$

Trade entry is triggered if and only if $p_t \ge q_t$, locking out further execution for that calendar day (Daily Top-1 Protocol).
